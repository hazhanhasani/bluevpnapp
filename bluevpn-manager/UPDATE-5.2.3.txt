BlueVPN 5.2.3 — Store Publishing Readiness

Android / Google Play
- Builds and permanently signs a Google Play AAB in GitHub Actions.
- Uses upstream v2rayNG 2.2.6 with targetSdk 37 and a fail-closed targetSdk >=36 gate.
- Google Play flavor is consumption-only: existing-account login and entitlement use only.
- Account creation, external digital checkout and external APK self-update are disabled in the Play build.
- Play merged manifest removes REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES and AD_ID.
- Tapsell SDK is excluded from the Play flavor; direct/F-Droid flavor retains its SDK implementation.
- Native libraries are checked for 16 KB ELF LOAD alignment and APK zip alignment.
- Public privacy/terms pages and Play Console/VpnService/Data Safety checklists are included.

Windows / Microsoft Store
- Adds a dedicated Microsoft Store EXE/MSI workflow mode (`store_release=true`).
- Store mode requires CA-trusted Authenticode secrets and fails closed without them.
- Every EXE/DLL PE in the self-contained payload is verified as Authenticode-valid before packaging.
- The offline Inno Setup installer is signed and verified, producing x64 and ARM64 Store-named installers.
- Partner Center checklist, certification notes, listing draft and signing-secret guide are included.

Validation
- Release, PHP and Windows validators must pass.
- Complete Python regression suite must pass.
- Store policy regression tests cover Play/AAB/16 KB and Microsoft Store Authenticode gates.
