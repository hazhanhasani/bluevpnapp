BlueVPN Site 4.16.9

- Elementor header/footer empty output uses the internal BlueVPN fallback without generating a Sentinel runtime error.
- A BlueVPN-managed header/footer template is self-healed only when its stored Elementor document is actually empty/corrupt.
- User-edited Elementor templates are never overwritten just because frontend rendering returns empty.
- Real Elementor exceptions remain Sentinel errors.
